  • Blocking mats and pins
  • An optimistic attitude and a very long weekend

Swatching Process

base.css → ol, li
  1. Chain 20 stitches in your chosen yarn and hook.
  2. Work 20 rows of your main stitch pattern.
  3. Bind off loosely and wet block the swatch.
  4. Let dry completely — at least 24 hours, resist the urge to check.
  5. Measure gauge in the centre of the swatch (edges lie).

📌 Note

If your gauge doesn't match after two hook sizes, the problem might be tension rather than hook size. Try consciously loosening your grip on both the hook and the yarn.

✨ Tip

Block your swatch the same way you plan to block your finished garment. If you're going to steam block the cardigan, steam block the swatch too — different methods can give very different results.

Pattern Abbreviations

All patterns in this series use US crochet terminology. The main stitch is the hdc (half double crochet), worked in a ch-2 turning chain.

Foundation: ch 61
Row 1: hdc in 3rd ch from hook, hdc across — 59 hdc
Row 2: ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across
Repeat Row 2 until piece measures 15" from foundation
