Cozy Cardigan – Part 1: Planning & Swatching

January 14, 2025 · Crochet Knitting

Starting my first cardigan — yarn choices, gauge swatches, and an ambitious timeline.

[hero image — yarn skeins on a wooden table]

Introduction

I've been knitting scarves and squares for years, and this winter I decided it was time to try a cardigan. A skein of rust-orange wool at the local shop sealed the deal. This is the log of what happened next.

I've tackled smaller garment pieces before, so I went in with a plan: good yarn, careful swatching, and a realistic timeline. Two out of three isn't bad.

Materials

After an afternoon at the yarn shop, I came home with the following:

  • 800g of worsted weight (weight 4) yarn — Sage Mist colourway
  • 5mm crochet hook (or size needed to achieve gauge)
  • Stitch markers — at least 10
  • Yarn needle for seaming
  • Blocking mats and T-pins

Gauge Swatches

The pattern calls for 15 hdc × 17 rows over 10cm. I swatched three hook sizes, blocked them all, and measured obsessively.

Swatching Process

  1. Chain 20 stitches in your chosen yarn and hook.
  2. Work 20 rows of your main stitch pattern.
  3. Bind off loosely and wet-block the swatch.
  4. Leave to dry completely — at least 24 hours. Resist the urge to check.
  5. Measure gauge at the centre, not the edges.

"The gauge swatch is not a suggestion. It is the entire project, miniaturised."

Note

If your gauge doesn't match after two hook sizes, the problem is probably tension rather than hook size. Try consciously loosening your grip on both the hook and the yarn.

Tip

Block your swatch the same way you plan to block the finished garment. Steam blocking and wet blocking give different results — measure after doing whichever method you'll use for the final piece.

Pattern Abbreviations

All posts in this series use US crochet terminology. The main stitch is hdc (half double crochet), worked into a ch-2 turning chain. 

Foundation: ch 61
Row 1: hdc in 3rd ch from hook, hdc across — 59 hdc
Row 2: ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across
Repeat Row 2 until piece measures 15" from foundation

What's Next

Gauge is confirmed, yarn is wound, and the pattern is marked up. In Part 2 I'll start the back panel.
